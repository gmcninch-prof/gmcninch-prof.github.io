This semester (Fall 2026), we'll run a seminar on **Formalization of math and Lean**
in the math department here at Tufts University.  

Schedule: weekly, Tuesday 3:30 - 4:20. Meeting in JCC 302.  

The first meeting will be 2026-09-15.

The [Lean prover community
website](https://leanprover-community.github.io/) has lots of Lean
resources.  In particular, probably the first thing you need to know
is how to [install Lean on your
computer](https://lean-lang.org/install/).

There is also a useful page of [Learning resources](https://leanprover-community.github.io/learn.html)

Two particularly useful "book-references" are

- [Mathematics in Lean](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathematics_in_lean/)
- [Theorem Proving in Lean](https://lean-lang.org/theorem_proving_in_lean4/)

I plan to post some [notes for the seminar here](https://gmcninch.math.tufts.edu/proof-sketches/posts).
